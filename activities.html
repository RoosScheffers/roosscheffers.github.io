Workshop on Cognitive and Empirical Research in Computational Argumentation (CERCA2026)

Workshop co-located with COMMA 2026. The goal of this workshop was to create a space for researchers working with empirical cognitive methods on argumentation to share materials, present ideas, and test studies. Sharing ideas will facilitate further studies, collaborations, and make it easier for interested researchers to get involved. Therefore, the workshop is also open to any researchers interested in the topic and those who have ideas that others might be able to test.
